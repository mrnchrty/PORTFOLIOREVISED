# About Me and Resume separation

- About Me: `portfolio/about/index.html`, `style.css`, `script.js`. Personal background, interests and hobbies remain here.
- Resume: `portfolio/resume/index.html`, `style.css`, `script.js`. Education, qualifications, leadership, technical and creative skills, and projects belong here.
- Shared navigation and styles remain under `shared/`. Links to both pages have been updated throughout text-based project files.
- The project previously already had two separate pages; this update standardizes the requested filenames and expands resume content.
- No verified employment history or PDF resume was provided; neither has been invented.
- `backend/.env` and `backend/node_modules` are intentionally excluded from the distributable ZIP. Run `npm install` inside `backend/` and configure from `.env.example`.
- Live authentication, payments and database integrations require configured credentials and environment testing; they are not claimed as verified.
